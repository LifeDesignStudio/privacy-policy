Privacy Policy — Qurio

Last Updated: May 14, 2026
Developer: Life Design Studio

1. Overview

Qurio is a knowledge exploration app that uses AI to answer curiosity-driven questions. The questions you type are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses. All exploration history and knowledge graph data are stored exclusively on your device.

2. Information Sent to Third Parties

When you submit a question, the text of that question is transmitted to Google's Gemini API for processing. This is necessary to generate the AI-powered responses that are central to the app's functionality. This transmission is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to the content of your questions or the responses generated.

3. Local Storage

The following information is stored exclusively on your device and is never accessible to the developer:

  • Knowledge graph — a record of topics you have explored, including question text, AI-generated responses, and topic tags, used to build your personal knowledge map.
  • Synapse history — cross-topic connections detected between your exploration sessions.
  • Badges — achievement records based on your exploration depth within topic categories.

4. Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking of any kind.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 12 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from any user. Questions submitted through the app are processed by Google's Gemini API and are subject to Google's terms of service and privacy policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

The developer reserves the right to modify this policy. Updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above.
